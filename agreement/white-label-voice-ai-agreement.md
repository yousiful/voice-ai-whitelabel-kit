## 1. Parties and what is licensed

1.1 Provider grants Client a non-exclusive, non-transferable license to use the Platform under the {{client_brand}} name and to resell access to its own customers ("Customers").

1.2 Setup scope. Provider will deliver: {{setup_scope}}.

1.3 Launch timeline. Provider will complete setup within {{launch_days}} days after the launch fee clears and Client has supplied the items Provider asks for (logo, domain, brand colors, payment details). Delays caused by Client move the date day for day.

1.4 Provider keeps all rights in the Platform, templates and prompts it supplies. Client keeps its brand, its Customer relationships and its Customer data.

## 3. Usage credits

3.1 Usage (voice minutes, phone numbers, SMS, email, AI tokens) is paid from a prepaid wallet. There is no usage billed after the fact.

3.2 Client loads at least ${{minimum_wallet_load}} before launch. Auto-reload is on. When the balance falls below ${{auto_reload_floor}}, the wallet reloads by ${{auto_reload_amount}} using the card or bank account on file.

3.3 When the balance reaches zero, voice, SMS and AI features pause until the wallet is reloaded. Provider is not liable for missed calls or messages during a pause.

3.4 Credits are non-refundable once loaded, except where required by law.

3.5 Provider may change usage rates on {{rate_change_notice_days}} days written notice. If a vendor raises its price, Provider may pass the increase through on {{vendor_increase_notice_days}} days written notice.

3.6 Provider reports usage totals each month. Provider's own vendor costs are confidential and are not disclosed.

## 4. Payment, support and suspension

4.1 Fees and credit reloads are collected by card or ACH autopay. Client authorizes Provider to charge the method on file.

4.2 Client pays Provider in full even if Client's Customers do not pay Client.

4.3 Client must dispute any charge in writing within {{dispute_days}} days of the charge. A chargeback filed without first using this dispute process is a breach of this agreement, and Client will also pay the chargeback fee and Provider's reasonable costs to answer it.

4.4 If any amount is {{suspension_days}} days past due, Provider may pause the account. Reactivation costs ${{reactivation_fee}} plus all amounts owed.

4.5 Support is available {{support_hours}}. Provider will respond to tickets within {{support_response_hours}} business hours. Support goes to Client, not to Client's Customers.

4.6 Provider does not guarantee sales, appointments, results, AI accuracy, or uninterrupted uptime. AI agents can make mistakes, and Client is responsible for reviewing scripts and call outcomes.

## 5. White label rights and non-circumvention

5.1 For {{noncircumvention_months}} months after the end of this agreement, Client will not go directly to Provider's vendors, staff or customers to obtain the Platform or its services, or help anyone else do so, in order to avoid paying Provider.

5.2 Because the harm is hard to measure, if Client breaches section 5.1, Client will pay liquidated damages equal to {{noncircumvention_months}} months of the platform fee at the rate in effect at the time of the breach. This is a reasonable estimate of loss and not a penalty.

5.3 Provider will not solicit Client's Customers to move to a competing brand during the same period.

## 6. Compliance and data

6.1 Client is responsible for compliance with the TCPA, Do Not Call rules, A2P 10DLC registration, call recording and consent laws, and any state or industry rules that apply to its calls and messages. Client must collect and keep proof of consent.

6.2 Client will defend and indemnify Provider against claims, fines and costs that come from Client's or its Customers' messages, calls, content, or breach of this section.

6.3 Each side keeps the other's non-public information confidential and uses it only for this agreement. This does not apply to information that is public, already known, or required to be disclosed by law.

6.4 Client also agrees to the terms of the underlying platform vendor and of carriers and messaging providers, as updated from time to time.

## 7. Term and exit

7.1 The minimum term is {{min_term_months}} months from the launch date. After that, the agreement continues month to month.

7.2 Either side may end the agreement after the minimum term with {{notice_days}} days written notice.

7.3 If Client ends the agreement before the minimum term, Client pays an early exit fee of ${{early_exit_fee}}.

7.4 If either side breaches this agreement, the other may end it if the breach is not fixed within {{cure_days}} days of written notice. Non-payment is a breach.

7.5 After the end date, Client has {{export_days}} days to export its data. After that, Provider may delete it.

7.6 Provider will transfer phone numbers and sub-accounts to Client only when all amounts owed are paid and Client pays a transfer fee of ${{transfer_fee}}.

## 8. Legal basics

8.1 Each side keeps its own intellectual property. No other rights are granted.

8.2 Each side's total liability under this agreement is limited to the fees paid in the {{liability_cap_months}} months before the claim. This cap does not apply to Client's payment obligations, Client's indemnity in section 6.2, breach of confidentiality, section 5 damages, fraud, or liability that cannot be limited by law.

8.3 Neither side is liable for lost profits or indirect damages, except as covered by the carve-outs above.

8.4 Changes to this agreement must be in writing and signed by both sides, except for rate changes under section 3.5.

8.5 This agreement is governed by the laws of {{governing_state}}. Disputes will be heard in the courts located in {{governing_state}}.

8.6 This is the whole agreement between the parties on this subject.
